About Glacier Pointe at Tehaleh

Now Selling – Homes in Bonney Lake WA at Tehaleh! Discover a nature-inspired way of life at Tehaleh, a master-planned community offering new homes in Bonney Lake, Washington. Surrounded by the beauty of the Pacific Northwest, Tehaleh features an extensive network of open space, parks, and trails designed to support an active and connected lifestyle. Garrette Homes offers spacious homesites ranging from approximately one-third to one-half acre, providing the opportunity to build a home that reflects your lifestyle while enjoying room to breathe. These larger homesites allow for flexibility, privacy, and a strong connection to the surrounding landscape. Outdoor living is central to life at Tehaleh. Residents enjoy access to more than 1,800 acres of preserved open space, over 40 miles of trails, and 14 thoughtfully planned parks. The upcoming Glacier Pointe neighborhood expands the community even further, offering a location just off Cascadia Boulevard East with closer proximity to sweeping views of Mount Rainier. As one of the most sought-after communities in the Bonney Lake area, Tehaleh offers a balance of natural surroundings and neighborhood amenities that make everyday living feel both peaceful and engaging. Whether you are drawn to quiet trails, vibrant gathering spaces, or scenic views, Tehaleh delivers a setting designed to support the way you want to live. Contact us today to learn more about available homesites, upcoming phases, and the opportunity to build your new home in Tehaleh in Bonney Lake, WA.
